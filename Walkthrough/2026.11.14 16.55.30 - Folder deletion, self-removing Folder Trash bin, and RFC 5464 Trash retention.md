# Folder deletion, self-removing Folder Trash bin, and RFC 5464 Trash retention

## Context
Users could create account group folders (`📁+ Group`) and auto-create local folders via filter rules, yet had no UI to create, rename, or delete mail folders (`MailFolderData`) or delete custom account group folders (`AccountFolderData`). In addition, neither deleted folders nor mail `Trash` folders had configurable auto-purge retention or server retention visibility.

## Observations & Decisions
1. **Self-deleting Folder Trash (`DeletedFolderStore`):** Soft-deleted mail folders (backed up with `.eml` messages and folder metadata) and non-default account group folders (preserving `OriginalParentFolderId` and `ReassignedAccountIds`) live under `UserData/DeletedFolders`. Whenever `entries.Count == 0` (at startup, restore, permanent delete, empty-all, or scheduled expiry), `DeletedFolderStore` removes `UserData/DeletedFolders` on disk and `MailSidebar` hides the `Folder Trash` / `Group Trash` rows completely while `MailUiState` returns Column 2 to `INBOX`.
2. **Configurable Folder Trash & Mail Trash retention (`TrashRetentionService`):**
   - Deleted folders default to `2` days (`Settings.AutoPurgeDeletedFolders = true`, `Settings.DeletedFolderRetentionDays = 2`), overridable per account via `InheritedSetting` in `AccountSettings`.
   - Mail `Trash` (`FolderRole.Trash`) defaults to `30` days (`Settings.AutoEmptyTrash = true`, `Settings.TrashRetentionDays = 30`), overridable per account (`AccountSettings`) and per folder (`FolderSettingsStore`). `MessageStore` stamps `TrashedAtUtc` when messages move into or first appear inside `Trash` and preserves it across sync index updates (`CarryOverLocalState`).
3. **Server Trash retention discovery & sync (`RFC 5464 IMAP METADATA`):** `ImapSyncService.SyncFolderListAsync` queries `ImapCapabilities.Metadata` (`GetMetadataAsync` on Cyrus/Dovecot `/private` and `/shared` `expire` entries) on `FolderRole.Trash` mailboxes, persisting `ServerRetentionDays` and `ServerSupportsRetentionMetadata` on `MailFolderData` (preserved across `MessageStore.SaveFolder`). `TrashRetentionService.PushTrashRetentionToServerInBackground` pushes updated retention days via `SetMetadataAsync` when supported; otherwise local auto-purge and server expiry coexist ("first to delete wins"). Both values render directly on the sidebar `Trash` row (`Srv Xd · App Yd` or `App Yd`) and in the `Trash` header banner.
4. **Global `CoreButtonCss` & `PillButton` availability (`DebugApi` visual catch):** `DebugApi` screenshot inspection of `Settings` and `Deleted Folders Trash` revealed that `<CoreButtonCss />` was previously mounted per `<CoreButton>` instance rather than in `MainLayout.razor`, leaving `<PillButton>` unstyled whenever a view or page section had no `<CoreButton>` mounted, and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` inside `CoreButtonCss` caused `"Cancel"` to wrap mid-word in narrow `ConfirmDialogModal` layouts. Mounting `<CoreButtonCss />` once in `MainLayout.razor` (including `.mail-search-toggle*` rules) and switching to `overflow-wrap: break-word` + `flex-wrap: wrap` resolved both across every page and modal.

## Verification
- **Runtime & Automated Checks:** `61/61` `FilterChecks` passed; `MyLovelyMail` and `MyLovelyMail.Web` built with `0` errors and `0` new warnings.
- **`DebugApi` Interactive & Visual Verification:** Verified creating, renaming, right-clicking, and deleting folders with `"Are you sure?"` confirmation; verified `Folder Trash` appearing with `2d` badge, restoring `YanlisKlasor` and emptying `Folder Trash` both deleting `UserData/DeletedFolders` on disk (`Test-Path == False`) and removing the sidebar row; verified `Srv 14d · App 7d` on the `Trash` folder row and `Auto-delete — Server: 14 day(s) · App: 7 day(s)` banner; verified `TrashRetentionModal` and `Settings -> Trash & Folder Retention` pill controls.
