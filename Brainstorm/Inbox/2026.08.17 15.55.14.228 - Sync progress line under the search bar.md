# Sync progress line under the search bar

Kategori: functional-win | Efor: M | Rank: 50

## Ne
SyncScheduler.cs already exposes static IsSyncing (line 16) but nothing about what it is doing. Add a static string SyncStatus (e.g. "Inbox esitleniyor (3/8)") plus a StatusChanged event on SyncScheduler; ImapSyncService and Pop3Service set it per folder/batch as they go. In Mail.razor, directly under the search input, render a 2px animated gradient bar (AppColors pink-to-lilac shimmer, keyframes emitted in MailCss.razor) with the status text beside it, subscribed in Mail.razor.cs via the existing ThemedComponentBase re-render / MainEvents pattern; collapses to height 0 when idle.

## Neden
Right now a sync is invisible until toasts fire; a live one-line status makes the app feel alive and matches the cafcafli direction with a shimmering progress ribbon, at low risk since the hooks (IsSyncing, per-folder loops) already exist.
