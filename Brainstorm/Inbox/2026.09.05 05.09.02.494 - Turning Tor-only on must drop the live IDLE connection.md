# Turning Tor-only on must drop the live IDLE connection

Kategori: protection | Efor: S | Rank: 2

## Ne
ImapIdleService.Refresh() builds its desired set from Enabled, Protocol and UseImapIdle only, and
never restarts a loop that is already running. AccountSettingsPage.SetTorOnly just saves the
account. So the IDLE client opened on the direct route keeps re-issuing IDLE every 9 minutes.
Fix: give ImapIdleService a way to drop one account loop (cancel its CTS and remove it, so the
next Refresh re-creates it) and call it when TorOnly changes.

## Neden
The user flips the account to Tor-only in the evening; the settings page reports a Tor route in
use; the socket opened directly that afternoon keeps talking to the provider in the clear all
night, until the next message happens to arrive.
