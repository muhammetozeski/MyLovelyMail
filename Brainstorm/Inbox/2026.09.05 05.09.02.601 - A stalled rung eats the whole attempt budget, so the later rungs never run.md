# A stalled rung eats the whole attempt budget, so the later rungs never run

Kategori: resilience | Efor: M | Rank: 3

## Ne
TorAttemptTimeout is 150s while Tor own SocksTimeout default is 2 minutes, and RunNetwork
re-executes the whole OpenAsync on every retry, so each attempt restarts at rung 1.
Fix: give each rung its own budget (a linked CTS per rung, well under the attempt timeout) so a
stalled one costs its share and not the whole pass.

## Neden
The proven endpoint accepts SOCKS but cannot attach the stream. Rung 1 eats about 120s, rung 2
gets 30s, Polly cancels, and the retry starts at rung 1 again - six times, up to twelve minutes,
without the app own SOCKS5 client, a rediscovered endpoint or a tor started by the app ever
being tried. The ladder stated purpose only holds for rungs that fail fast.
