# Certificate revocation fetches of a Tor-only account bypass the proxy

Kategori: protection | Efor: S | Rank: 1

## Ne
MailConnections.OpenThroughTorAsync sets ProxyClient and Timeout and nothing else.
CheckCertificateRevocation is never touched anywhere in MainProject and MailKit defaults it to
true (verified at runtime: ImapClient and SmtpClient both report True). With it on, SslStream
validates the chain with ONLINE revocation, which on Windows means CryptoAPI fetching the OCSP or
CRL URL over WinHTTP - a path that knows nothing about the SOCKS proxy.
Fix: set CheckCertificateRevocation = false on a Tor-only client, with the reason written down.
The check is not free to keep: it is the one part of the handshake that deliberately leaves the
tunnel.

## Neden
Two failures, opposite in shape. (a) The TCP stream is tunnelled, then the machine sends an OCSP
request carrying the mail server certificate serial to the CA responder, in the clear, resolving
the responder name through system DNS - the provider identity leaves the machine anyway. (b) On
the usual Tor-only setup, where non-Tor traffic is blocked at the firewall, the fetch cannot
complete, the chain builds RevocationStatusUnknown, MailKit rejects it, and EVERY rung of the
ladder ends in SslHandshakeException - the account never connects at all.
