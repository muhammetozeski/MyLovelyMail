# "No tor executable" is retried forever although three comments say it is not

Kategori: resilience | Efor: S | Rank: 2

## Ne
TorProcess.StartAsync throws TorUnavailableException without passing recoverable:false, and the
parameter defaults to true. StartOwnTorAsync rethrows it unchanged, the ladder short-circuit needs
!ex.Recoverable, the summary thrown afterwards is recoverable again, and both
ResiliencePolicy.IsWorthRetrying and ImapIdleService filter on Recoverable:false. Three comments
(TorService.cs, ResiliencePolicy.cs, ImapIdleService.cs) describe the opposite behaviour.
Fix: throw it with recoverable:false, and carry that flag through the ladder summary.

## Neden
On a machine with no tor, the wizard test runs all six rungs and then Polly retries six more times
with jittered 3/6/12/24/48/96s delays - minutes of spinner before a message that was knowable at
once. Every sync pass repeats it, and the IDLE loop reconnects forever at up to two-minute
intervals, rotating the circuit epoch and logging a full route report each time.
