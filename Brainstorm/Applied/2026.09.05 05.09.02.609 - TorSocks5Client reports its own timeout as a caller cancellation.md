# TorSocks5Client reports its own timeout as a caller cancellation

Kategori: resilience | Efor: S | Rank: 3

## Ne
The timeout overload of TorSocks5Client.ConnectAsync cancels a linked token, so the caller sees
OperationCanceledException where MailKit Socks5Client raises TimeoutException (verified side by
side). MailConnections catches OperationCanceledException with no token filter and aborts the
remaining rungs.
Fix: translate a budget expiry that the caller did not ask for into TimeoutException, and filter
the ladder catch on cancellationToken.IsCancellationRequested.

## Neden
Inside RunNetwork this is masked, but ImapIdleService, the POP3 parallel sessions and the debug
connect-test all call OpenXAsync directly. The IDLE loop reaches rung 3 against a stalled proxy,
gives up there 240s later, records "The operation was canceled." as the account health, and never
tries rungs 4 to 6 in that iteration.
