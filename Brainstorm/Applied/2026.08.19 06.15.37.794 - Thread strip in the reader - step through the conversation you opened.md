# Thread strip in the reader: step through the conversation you opened

Kategori: reading-pane navigation | Efor: M | Rank: 1

## Ne
Add a conversation strip under the reader header in `MainProject/UI/Pages/Mail.razor` (between the `.mail-reader-header` div that closes around line 458 and the `.mail-reader-actions` div at line 461) showing `◀ 3 of 7 ▶` plus one compact line per message (date · sender · unread dot); clicking a line calls the existing `void OpenMessage(MailMessageSummary)` at `MainProject/UI/Pages/Mail.razor.Reader.cs:265`. Compute it in `Mail.razor.Reader.cs` with the same uid-keyed caching pattern `HistoryOfOpenSender`/`senderHistoryUid` uses (`Mail.razor.Reader.cs:51-81`) — fields `openThread` + `openThreadUid`, built from `ThreadingService.BuildThreads(MessageStore.GetSummaries(account.Id, ResolveFolderOf(open)))` picking the `MailThread` whose `Messages` contain the open uid. Feed it the folder's full summaries, NOT `FilteredSummaries`, or an active search truncates the thread. Two details the idea as written misses: (1) render the strip whenever the computed thread has more than one message, not only when `ConversationMode` is on — a thread exists in flat mode too and the strip is just as useful there; (2) opening a non-newest message puts `MailUiState.OpenMessage` outside `Rows`, so the `mail-row--active` highlight at `Mail.razor:243` drops off — decide deliberately (keep the thread's newest row marked active, or accept no highlight) rather than discovering it as a visual bug. Add `n`/`p` to the `switch (e.Key)` in `HandleListKeyDown` (`Mail.razor.Interaction.cs:198`, both keys currently free) guarded on `MailUiState.OpenMessage != null`, plus two rows in `ShortcutHelpRows` (`Mail.razor.Interaction.cs:18`). Styling goes next to `.mail-reader-history` (`MainProject/UI/Pages/MailCss.razor:560`), reusing `AppMeasures.Font.Small` and the `--account-accent` variable `.mail-screen` already sets (`Mail.razor:12`). Verify through the DebugApi only: `GET /threads?accountId=&folder=` (already returns `count` and `uids`) to find a thread with `count > 1`, `POST /open` its newest uid, `GET /dom?selector=.mail-reader-thread` to confirm the strip and all N rows render; add a small `POST /reader/thread-step?delta=-1` endpoint and an `openMessageUid` field on `GET /status` so the step is assertable without touching the screen.

## Neden
Conversation grouping is on by default (`Settings.ConversationView = new(true)`) and `BuildRows()` at `Mail.razor.cs:150-156` maps each thread to `thread.Newest` alone, so in a seven-message thread six messages have no row at all — while the `mail-row-thread-count` pill at `Mail.razor:256` advertises that they exist. The only ways out today are toggling 💬 Conversations off or typing a free-text search (`ConversationMode` requires `!SearchService.HasFreeText(SearchText)`, which silently drops the list to flat), and neither is reachable from the reader — which is exactly where you are when you need to walk a back-and-forth backwards. This is a functional hole in the client's core job, not a comfort feature, and every piece it needs already exists: `ThreadingService`, `MailThread.Messages` ordered oldest-first, `MailUiState.OpenMessageInReader`, `ResolveFolderOf`, and the `/threads` + `/open` + `/dom` endpoints. Wiring, not new machinery.
