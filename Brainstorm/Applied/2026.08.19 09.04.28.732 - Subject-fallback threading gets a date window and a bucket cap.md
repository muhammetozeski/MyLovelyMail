# Subject-fallback threading gets a date window and a bucket cap

Kategori: threading | Efor: M | Rank: 2

## Ne
MainProject/Services/Mail/ThreadingService.cs:60-78 pass 2 keeps Dictionary<string,int> indexBySubject holding only the FIRST index for a normalized subject key, then unions any later reply-looking message into it with no date or size limit. Change the value to a small bucket list (List<int> per key) and union only when the candidate is within 30 days of some member of the bucket and the bucket is under a cap of 50; past either limit, open a NEW bucket under the same key instead of merging. Pass 1 (Message-Id / InReplyTo / ReferenceIds union-find, lines 52-55) stays untouched, so real References-linked threads keep merging without limit. Add POST /threads/probe to MainProject/zTests/DebugApi.cs taking a JSON array of synthetic summaries (subject, date, messageId) and returning the resulting thread sizes plus each thread's date span, following the ('POST', '/rule-audit/probe') case at DebugApi.cs:280 and ('POST', '/mute/incoming-probe') at :463 already in RouteAsync. Scope stops there: the sender-line rendering in Mail.razor.cs:151-162 is a separate cosmetic concern and is not part of this change.

## Neden
A folder of identical subjects breaks the grouping itself, not just its looks. Automated mail (a nightly 'Re: Backup report', a shop's 'RE: Your order') carries a fresh Message-Id and no References, so replyLike is true for every one of them and they all union into index 0. Conversation grouping is on by default (Stores/Settings.cs:94 ConversationView = true), so three years of a daily job renders as ONE row with a 1095 count pill at Mail.razor:274-277, a reader conversation strip whose N/P stepping walks 1095 entries, and 1094 messages that are simply not in the list. Gmail's own subject fallback is windowed for exactly this reason. Verifiable in one sitting with no mail server: POST 400 probe summaries sharing one 'Re:' subject a day apart and read the returned thread sizes (before: one thread of 400; after: buckets bounded by the 30-day window and the 50 cap), then run GET /threads?accountId=...&folder=INBOX&take=20 against the real cache and confirm genuine References-linked conversations group exactly as before.
