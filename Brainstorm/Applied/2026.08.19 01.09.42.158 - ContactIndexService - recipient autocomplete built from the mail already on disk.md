Verify with a new case ("GET", "/contacts") in MainProject/zTests/DebugApi.cs alongside the existing /threads and /compose cases, taking accountId/prefix/take: POST /sync against the local mail server, then assert Sent-weighted ordering, the dedupe, and the absence of the account's own address.

## Neden
Every address the user has mailed or been mailed by is already in the RAM summary index, yet compose makes them retype it into a plain text field - the app's most likely typo source. Verified absent: no Contact, datalist, or autocomplete code exists anywhere in MainProject. Reuses the exact SearchService iteration, so no new storage, no new sync path, and the endpoint makes it verifiable without touching the screen.
