# Theme cards become live mail-screen miniatures

Kategori: settings flash | Efor: M | Rank: 50

## Ne
Upgrade each theme card in Settings.razor (the AppThemes.All loop, lines 13-25) from name+3 dots into a tiny fake mail screen rendered purely with divs: a slim sidebar strip in theme.Primary, two or three rounded 'mail row' bars in theme.SurfaceSubtle/Secondary tones with a small unread dot in theme.Accent, all colored inline from the AppTheme's own properties (same pattern as the existing ThemePreviewStyle helper). Add a MiniPreviewStyle-family of static helpers in Settings.razor's @code block and new .settings-theme-mini* rules in SettingsCss.razor. The active card keeps its ShadowPrimaryGlow ring; on hover the miniature's unread dot pulses via a keyframe.

## Neden
Three color dots say nothing about how a theme actually feels; a miniature inbox lets the user pick a theme at a glance and is exactly the kind of cafcafli showcase moment the user asked for. Pure markup+inline styles from existing AppTheme data, no new services or JS.
