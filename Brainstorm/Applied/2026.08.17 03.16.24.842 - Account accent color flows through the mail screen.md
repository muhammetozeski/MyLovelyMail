# Account accent color flows through the mail screen

Kategori: lovely design delight | Efor: S | Rank: 20

## Ne
MailAccountData.ColorHex already exists but is barely used. In Mail.razor, set style="--account-accent: @MailUiState.SelectedAccount?.ColorHex" on the mail-page root div; in MailCss.razor switch selection highlight (.mail-row--active), unread chip background, folder-list selected state, reading-pane header underline, and the search box focus ring to var(--account-accent, <AppColors fallback>). Add a tiny color swatch row (6-8 pastel presets) to the AccountWizard so users actually pick one.

## Neden
With multiple accounts the whole workspace subtly re-tints when you switch accounts — instant orientation plus personality, exactly the 'account color theming' delight. Zero new state: the property, MailUiState.SelectedAccount, and the CSS variable mechanism all exist; it is wiring, not building.
