## Neden
Every reply carries the entire history forward with a flat `> ` prefix, so a five-round thread ships a body several times larger than what was actually written, and the only way to shorten it is hand-deleting lines in the textarea. The reader side already folds quoted history behind `FoldQuotedText` -- the app hides the bloat it keeps producing, with no matching control on the write side. Trimmed mode also shrinks what `StoreAsMessageInLocalFolderAsync` writes into Sent for every outgoing message, and every piece of it is a pure function exercisable through the DebugApi.
