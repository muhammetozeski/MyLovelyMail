# Filter and rules engine

Kategori: ux | Efor: L | Rank: 8

## Ne
FilterRule model: conditions (from, to, subject, body, size, has-attachment, regex, AND-OR groups) + actions (move to local or remote folder, mark important or read, mute, custom notification sound, tag, delete, forward). RuleEngine runs on new mail and on demand. Rules stored in UserData, editable UI page.

## Neden
Explicit user requirement: dozens of filter criteria with per-filter notification behavior.
