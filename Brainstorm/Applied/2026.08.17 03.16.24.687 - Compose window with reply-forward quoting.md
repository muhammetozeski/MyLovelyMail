# Compose window with reply-forward quoting

Kategori: composing | Efor: L | Rank: 20

## Ne
A ComposePane (CoreModal-hosted or third view state in Mail.razor via MailUiState) with To/Cc/Subject CoreInput fields and a plain multiline body textarea. Reply/Reply-All/Forward IconButtons in the reading pane prefill recipients, 'Re:/Fwd:' subject, and a '> '-quoted body loaded from MessageStore's on-disk MIME (MimeMessage.Load, TextBody). Send goes through the existing SmtpSendService with the account's saved identity; on success the sent MimeMessage is written into MessageStore under the Sent folder.

## Neden
The app can receive and sync mail but cannot write any yet; this is the smallest end-to-end compose path and the anchor every later compose feature (drafts, signatures, undo send) hangs off. It reuses SmtpSendService, MessageStore, and the Core element set with no new dependencies.
