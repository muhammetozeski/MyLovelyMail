# Reader open animation with replay-on-switch and gradient subject

Kategori: reader flash | Efor: M | Rank: 50

## Ne
In Mail.razor wrap the open-message reader content (the block starting at the mail-reader-header around line 218) in a container with @key="open.Id" so Blazor recreates it on every message switch, and give it class mail-reader-enter. In MailCss.razor add @@keyframes mail-reader-enter (opacity 0 -> 1, translateY(10px) scale(0.985) -> none, ~260ms cubic-bezier ease-out) applied to .mail-reader-enter, mirroring the existing pageFadeSlideIn pattern from MainLayoutCss.razor. In the same pass restyle .mail-reader-subject (MailCss.razor line 446): replace the plain color with background: linear-gradient(90deg, var(--account-accent, primary), a second AppColors pastel), -webkit-background-clip: text and transparent text fill, keeping the existing accent underline. Because the @key recreates the node, the gradient subject and body slide in together every time a mail is opened.

## Neden
Opening a mail is the single most repeated interaction and currently the reader pane just snaps to new content. A keyed enter animation plus a gradient headline is the flashiest visible win per line of CSS, uses only existing theme constants, and touches exactly two files (Mail.razor, MailCss.razor).
