# IMAP IDLE instant push

Kategori: resilience | Efor: M | Rank: 12

## Ne
Keep a persistent ImapClient per enabled IMAP account running IDLE on the Inbox while UseImapIdle is on; on server notification trigger an incremental SyncOpenedFolderAsync pass. Reconnect with backoff via ResiliencePolicy when the connection drops; stop cleanly on Settings change.

## Neden
New mail should arrive instantly instead of waiting for the SyncIntervalMinutes timer.

## Uygulama notu
Shipped 2026-08-17 as ImapIdleService: per-account IDLE loop, CountChanged cancels the done-token for instant wake, reconnect backoff 2s-2min, IDLE-unsupported hosts remembered so they stop reconnecting. Verified live on Gmail: send-to-toast in 7 seconds.
