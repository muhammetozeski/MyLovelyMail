# ThreadingService with References capture in sync

Kategori: threading-core | Efor: M | Rank: 60

## Ne
Add `InReplyTo` (string) and `ReferenceIds` (List<string>) to MailMessageSummary (MainProject/DataModels/Mail/MailMessageSummary.cs) and populate them in ImapSyncService.cs (~line 230, from item.Envelope.InReplyTo — fetch References via the Headers/BODY.PEEK summary items) and Pop3Service.cs. Then a new static `ThreadingService` (MainProject/Services/Mail/ThreadingService.cs) with `BuildThreads(List<MailMessageSummary>) -> List<MailThread>`: union-find over MessageId/InReplyTo/References links, falling back to a normalized-subject key (strip Re:/Fwd:/Fw:/[tag] prefixes with a pattern added to RegexConstants.cs). MailThread holds ordered summaries, newest date, unread count, participant addresses.

## Neden
MessageId is captured today but nothing links replies together; this is the single data+algorithm prerequisite every threading UI needs, and it fits the RAM-summary architecture (no disk MIME reads, pure in-memory grouping).
