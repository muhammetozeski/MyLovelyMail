# Aurora drift - slowly breathing backdrop

Kategori: app shell flash | Efor: M | Rank: 50

## Ne
Animate the existing aurora backdrop. Move the radial stack from AppStyles.BuildAuroraBackground() onto a fixed full-viewport `body::before` layer (sized ~130vw/130vh, overflow hidden by body) in MainLayoutCss.razor, keep the base linear-gradient on body itself. Add a `@@keyframes aurora-drift` that slowly translates/scales the layer (e.g. translate(-3%, -2%) scale(1.06) at 50%) plus a subtle `filter: hue-rotate(8deg)` swing, duration from a new AppMeasures.Duration.Glacial (~18-25s), ease-in-out, infinite alternate. Emit the pseudo-element CSS from a new AppStyles.BuildAuroraLayer() so the stops still come from AppColors.AuroraStops. Wrap in `@@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`.

## Neden
The aurora is currently a frozen still image; giving it a barely-perceptible drift is the single biggest 'cafcafli' upgrade because it touches every screen at once, costs zero markup changes, and pure transform/filter animation stays GPU-composited so it will not jank the mail list.
